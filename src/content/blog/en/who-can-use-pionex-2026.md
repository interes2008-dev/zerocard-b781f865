Search for Pionex from Berlin, Chicago and Bangalore and you'll end up in three different places. That isn't a glitch. Over the last year the exchange has split along regulatory lines, and the platform you can legally use now depends on your country of residence.

## Three platforms under one family

**pionex.com** is the global platform: trading bots, the Pionex Card, 0.05% spot fees. This is the one most reviews describe.

**Webot in the US.** Pionex.US was renamed Webot. It holds US money transmitter registrations and runs its own set of built-in bots.

**Webot in the EU.** Pionew Ireland Limited, trading as Webot, is authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland as a crypto-asset service provider under MiCA and as a payment institution, with passporting into other EEA countries.

## Why the EU split matters now

MiCA's transitional period ended on 1 July 2026. From that date, ESMA says, any crypto provider without authorisation must have wound down its EU business, and firms outside the EU can't serve EU residents except in narrow reverse-solicitation cases.

ESMA's advice to users is direct: check that your provider appears in the MiCA register, and if it doesn't, move your assets to an authorised provider or your own wallet.

For an EU resident that means Webot's EU platform, not pionex.com. France had already added pionex.com to its regulator's blacklist back in April 2025.

## Countries where pionex.com won't verify you

Pionex doesn't support phone numbers or identity checks for residents of:

- the US, Canada, the UK, France, the Netherlands, Austria;
- Japan, Singapore, China, Hong Kong;
- Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk;
- sanctioned or unstable regions such as Iran, North Korea, Cuba, Lebanon, Libya, Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, Yemen, Venezuela, Afghanistan, DR Congo, Zimbabwe.

Using a VPN to get around this is a reliable way to get an account frozen: the IP, phone number and ID have to line up during verification.

## Where pionex.com is a straightforward choice

If you live outside the EU and outside the list above, pionex.com is open to you. That covers most of Latin America, Africa, the Middle East and Asia: India, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Turkey, the UAE, Brazil, Mexico, Argentina and many others.

Two separate questions are worth keeping apart:

1. **Can you open an account?** That's the verification list above.
2. **Will the card work at a given shop?** That's a different list. Pionex declines card payments at merchants tied to Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Iran, Venezuela, Myanmar, Afghanistan and North Korea. Details are in the [Pionex Card review](/en/blog/pionex-card-review).
