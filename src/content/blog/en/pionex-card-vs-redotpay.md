Pionex Card and RedotPay come up together whenever someone looks for a crypto card for travel and foreign subscriptions. Both let you spend stablecoins anywhere Visa or Mastercard works. They're built for different people, though, and the cheaper one depends on how you spend.

## The numbers

| | Pionex Card | RedotPay |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual card | free | 10 USDT |
| Physical card | by invitation, free | 100 USDT |
| Fee per purchase | Visa 1%, Mastercard up to 3.5% | about 1.2% FX, up to 2.2% with conversion per Pionex |
| Cashback | up to 1% | none |
| Interest on balance | 5% APR, paid hourly | none |
| Funding | USDT only | USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH and others |
| Purchase limit | Visa 10,000 USDT, Mastercard 20,000 | much higher, Pionex cites up to $100,000 per transaction |
| ATM | Visa 2% plus the ATM's fee | 2% up to $10,000 a month, 3% above |

Pionex figures come from its card review, last verified on September 3, 2026. RedotPay figures come from CryptoSlate's 2026 review and from Pionex's own comparison. That comparison is written by Pionex, so read it knowing who wrote it.

## Where the Pionex Card wins

**Everyday spending.** On Visa, the 1% fee and up to 1% cashback roughly cancel out. RedotPay pays no cashback, so every foreign-currency purchase costs a bit over a percent.

**Money that sits.** Pionex pays 5% a year on the card balance, credited hourly. 1,000 USDT parked there earns about $4 a month. A RedotPay balance earns nothing.

**Getting started.** The Pionex virtual card is free to issue.

## Where RedotPay wins

**More coins.** RedotPay can spend USDC, bitcoin and ether directly. If your savings aren't in USDT, you skip a sell step.

**Big payments.** Its limits are far higher, which matters for equipment, business travel or supplier invoices.

**A physical card for anyone.** Anyone can order RedotPay plastic for 100 USDT. Pionex hands out physical Visa cards only by invitation.

## A month, side by side

You spend $1,000 a month in local currency and keep 1,000 USDT on the card.

- **Pionex Visa:** $10 fee, up to $10 cashback, about $4 interest. Roughly +$4.
- **Pionex Mastercard**, worst case from Pionex's own pages: $35 fee, $1 cashback, about $4 interest. Roughly −$30.
- **RedotPay:** about $12 in fees at 1.2%, no cashback, no interest. Roughly −$12.

## Availability

Neither card works everywhere. RedotPay lists 42 restricted countries, Russia among them, according to CryptoSlate. Pionex doesn't verify residents of the US, Canada, the UK, Japan, Singapore and several others, and since MiCA it doesn't serve the EU. Check your country first: [who can sign up to Pionex in 2026](/en/blog/who-can-use-pionex-2026).

## Which to pick

If you can get the Pionex Visa and spend normal amounts, it costs less and your balance earns interest. RedotPay is the better tool for people who hold several coins or pay large invoices. If Pionex only offers you the Mastercard, compare honestly: for heavy local-currency spending RedotPay can come out cheaper.
