Pionex is running a campaign for its card holders: pay for an AI subscription with the Pionex Card and you get a shot at having the whole payment refunded, up to 50 USDT. Here's what's known about it, what the payment itself costs, and where the catches are.

## What the campaign offers

The campaign page puts it plainly: a full refund of your latest AI subscription payment, up to 50 USDT per payment. It runs on attempts: paying an AI service with the card counts as a completed task and earns you an attempt. The prize feed shows two outcomes, a 5 USDT cashback and a full subscription refund.

Services Pionex lists as partners:

- OpenAI / ChatGPT
- Anthropic / Claude
- Cursor
- xAI / Grok
- Suno
- ElevenLabs

According to Pionex, payments to other AI services count too.

Dates, the number of attempts and the full rules are on the [campaign page](https://www.pionex.com/en/activities/galaxy/paycard-ai-lottery). Check them there: campaign terms change, and we won't restate details we can't verify.

## What paying costs

Even without a prize, AI subscriptions are a good fit for the Pionex Card because they're billed in dollars.

- The card balance is USDT, which Pionex treats one to one with the dollar.
- Dollar purchases carry no conversion fee.
- Eligible purchases earn up to 1% cashback.

A $20 subscription costs exactly 20 USDT and returns about 0.2 USDT. The full Visa vs Mastercard breakdown is in the [Pionex Card review](/en/blog/pionex-card-review).

## Who it suits

The card works at merchants Pionex doesn't restrict. OpenAI and Anthropic are US companies, so the card side of the payment goes through.

The services have their own rules, though. Use ChatGPT or Claude only from a country on the provider's supported list. OpenAI states that using its services from outside that list can get an account blocked. You also need to live somewhere Pionex accepts sign-ups; see [who can sign up to Pionex in 2026](/en/blog/who-can-use-pionex-2026).

## If the payment is declined

Pionex added a section on declined payments to the campaign page. The usual causes for cards like this are familiar:

- **Low card balance.** Some services place a test charge when you link a card. Keep a little more than the subscription price on the card.
- **Card not in Apple Pay or Google Pay.** Some services on mobile only accept wallet payments. Visa adds to Apple Pay in one tap from the Pionex app.
- **Address mismatch.** Enter the billing address exactly as the Pionex app shows it.
- **Merchant rejects prepaid cards.** Only the service's support or a different card can fix that.

## How to take part

1. Sign up to Pionex and complete level 2 verification.
2. Get the card in the card section (Visa if possible) and top up the card account with USDT.
3. Link the card to your AI subscription.
4. Confirm on the campaign page that you're participating, then use your attempts.
