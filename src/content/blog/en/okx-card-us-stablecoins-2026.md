OKX has launched a virtual Mastercard for eligible US customers, backed by USDC, USDT and USDG in OKX Pay. The interesting change is not just another crypto card. It is the use of stablecoins as collateral for a charge card.

This explainer uses official OKX pages checked on November 16, 2026. It covers the US programme only, not similarly named products elsewhere.

## A charge card, not a crypto debit card

The [official launch announcement](https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/okx-card-us) describes a Mastercard issued by WebBank. It is virtual-only at launch and can be added to Apple Pay or Google Pay.

OKX's [product FAQ](https://www.okx.com/en-us/help/us-okx-card-what-is-the-okx-card) calls it a **secured charge card**. Stablecoins secure spending, and the full statement balance is due each billing cycle. That differs from a prepaid card loaded with fiat or a revolving credit card.

Look, the payment model matters. A wallet balance, reserved collateral and a bill to repay are not interchangeable.

## What happens when you pay

1. Hold supported stablecoins in an active OKX Pay account.
2. When a purchase is authorised, the required stablecoins are reserved as collateral.
3. Repay through Autopay or from your linked USD balance under the programme terms.

The ability to keep your crypto depends on the repayment path. Autopay should not be read as a promise that collateral is never sold. Read the repayment explanation before relying on a "spend without selling" headline.

## Who actually gets 10% cashback?

The [published tier table](https://www.okx.com/en-us/card) starts Classic customers at 2%, with a monthly cashback cap of 50 USDG. The headline 10% belongs to Infinite. Its displayed qualification paths include a $500,000 Pay balance, $100,000 card spend or VIP 4+. Those are not ordinary entry-level conditions.

By the way, supported USDT funding does not mean USDT purchases qualify for this reward. The [cashback FAQ](https://www.okx.com/en-us/help/us-okx-card-cashback-program-faq) requires eligible **USDG-backed purchases on Autopay**. Manual repayment does not qualify. When collateral is mixed, only the USDG-backed portion earns cashback.

For example, $500 of qualifying Classic purchases at 2% earns 10 USDG before the monthly cap is reached. A purchase backed entirely by USDT does not meet that USDG requirement. Excluded merchant categories and other conditions still apply.

## US eligibility comes first

The announcement addresses eligible US customers. It is not proof that someone in Europe, Russia or another country can apply. Merchant acceptance abroad and the right to obtain the card are separate questions.

Check the current regional eligibility terms before transferring funds. Do not use a false address or somebody else's documents. A Mastercard logo also cannot guarantee approval by every merchant.
