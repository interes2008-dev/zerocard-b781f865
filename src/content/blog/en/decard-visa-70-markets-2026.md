DCS DeCard announced an expanded collaboration with Visa on November 12, 2026. It says the agreement extends stablecoin-linked card issuing capabilities beyond Asia Pacific to more than 70 markets, including Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Latin America.

For people paid in digital dollars, that is worth watching. It is not a statement that every resident of those markets can already order a card.

## Two parts of the announcement

The [official DeCard release](https://www.thedecard.com/en/business/resources/news/dcs-decard-visa-global-expansion) identifies two business areas:

- Cards-as-a-Service, allowing fintechs and digital asset platforms to launch branded stablecoin-linked card programmes;
- DeCard Business Account, its corporate card and spend management platform, being expanded for stablecoin-enabled corporate payments.

Look, this is broader than a single consumer card launch. It is also infrastructure other companies can use to build payment products.

[The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/crypto-web3-and-cbdc/news/dcs-decard-expands-visa-partnership-for-stablecoin-linked-cards) reports that partners receive support across programme development, issuance, transaction processing and operations. That corroborates the announced business model. It is not an independent test of card availability in all 70-plus markets.

## What 70+ markets does not establish

| Confirmed in the announcement | Not automatically confirmed |
|---|---|
| Wider card issuing capabilities | Every consumer can apply today |
| Visa collaboration | Every merchant will approve every payment |
| Stablecoin-linked programmes | Every card accepts USDT on every network |
| Corporate spending expansion | Every freelancer qualifies for a business account |

The checked release does not provide a full universal consumer tariff or complete country-by-country application list. We cannot infer free issuance, a cashback rate or eligibility for a particular nationality from it.

## Why freelancers and travellers should care

A card funded from stablecoins can connect a digital-dollar income stream to ordinary purchases. Whether it is cheaper depends on conversion, funding, spending and withdrawal costs together.

By the way, personal and corporate programmes have different purposes. Businesses may need employee spending controls and transaction records. Travellers usually care more about residence eligibility, spending currency and mobile-wallet support.

Keep those requirements separate when comparing cards. A business infrastructure announcement is not a promise of an instantly available personal travel card.

## Before sending funds

1. Identify the issuer and the programme available in your country.
2. Confirm residence and identity-document requirements.
3. Check the supported funding asset and blockchain network.
4. Add up transfer, conversion and foreign-currency purchase costs.
5. Read refund handling and how to withdraw unused funds.

Never send USDT over a network the receiving programme does not support. A partnership release cannot replace the deposit instructions shown in the actual application.
